What I'm working on.

Studying how solar storms shake the edge of Earth's atmosphere — and the papers behind the work so far.

2025 – Present

How do solar storms change Earth's upper-atmosphere glow?

Geomagnetic storms disturb the thin hydrogen halo around Earth. I simulate the upper atmosphere with the NRLMSIS model and test it against Lyman-alpha measurements from the GOLD satellite, to understand how hydrogen escapes during a storm.
